# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Six color themes (Paper, Sky, Mint, Apricot, Lilac, Lemon) on a new "Make it yours" screen,
  opened from the sliders button on Home. Passing screens stay dark in every theme.
- Custom word lists: create, rename, add words one at a time or paste a whole list, remove
  words, hide from Home, copy as text, delete. Lists with 5+ words appear as categories on Home,
  and the card's category corner shows the list's name.
- Motion everywhere: buttons and chips press in, switches slide, screens ease in, the clue order
  slides in one by one, the hold target breathes, the card face eases in (and hides instantly),
  and the CAUGHT stamp lands on the result. Turned off when iOS "Reduce Motion" is on.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- First playable round: players and house rules, private hold-to-reveal cards, clue order,
  vote, result with the Imposter's guess, new round with the same table.
- 7 built-in categories (177 words), no repeats until a category runs out.
- Installable web app: works offline, Home Screen icon.
